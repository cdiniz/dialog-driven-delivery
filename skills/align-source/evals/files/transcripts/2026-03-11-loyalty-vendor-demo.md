# 2026-03-11 PointStack Vendor Demo

**Marco:** This is the PointStack demo. To be clear up front — this is an evaluation of their platform, it's not a design session for anything we're building ourselves.

**Vendor (Elise):** Happy to walk through it. PointStack is a hosted loyalty platform. Points, tiers, redemption, campaign tooling, all managed.

**Sam:** Talk about the tier model.

**Elise:** Three tiers out of the box — Bronze, Silver, Gold — with thresholds you configure. Members move up on trailing twelve-month spend and drop down after a lapse window.

**Sam:** Can you run it without tiers?

**Elise:** You can, but most of the retention lift our customers see comes from the tier mechanics, so we'd steer you toward using them.

**Nadia:** What does it cost?

**Elise:** It's priced per monthly active member. Four cents per MAU on the standard plan, with a floor of two thousand a month.

**Nadia:** And that floor applies from day one, before we have any volume?

**Elise:** From signature, yes.

**Marco:** That's the part that worries me. We'd be paying the floor for months before the member base justifies it.

**Elise:** Understood. Happy to leave the sandbox open for a few weeks.
